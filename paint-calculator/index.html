How Paint Calculation Works

Paint quantity depends on the surface area, the product's coverage rate, and the number of coats. A small extra allowance helps account for texture, application method and touch-ups.

Key Factors

  • Surface type and texture
  • Paint type and finish
  • Manufacturer coverage rate
  • Number of coats
  • Application method
  • Color change and primer

Tips for Accurate Results

  • Read the actual coverage rate on the paint can.
  • Include the full area of walls and ceilings.
  • Allow for a second coat when needed.
  • Consider textured or porous surfaces.
  • Keep paint from the same batch for touch-ups.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet does a gallon of paint cover?

Many paints specify a coverage range around 300–400 square feet per gallon for a single coat, but check your exact product label.

Should I plan for two coats?

Two coats are common for an even finish. The ideal number depends on the old and new colors, paint type and surface.

How much extra paint should I buy?

An allowance around 10–15% can be a useful planning starting point, depending on surface and application.
