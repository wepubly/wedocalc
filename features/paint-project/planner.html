Detailed measurements

Four rectangular walls. Do not include doors or windows you intend to paint. Counts share the entered size; use measured total area above for irregular layouts.

Methodology and assumptions

Gross wall area = 2 × (length + width) × height. Deduct count × width × height for doors and windows. Ceiling area = length × width, calculated separately. Coating quantity = net area × coats ÷ label coverage × (1 + allowance ÷ 100). Primer uses its own coverage and coats. Metric values convert through 1 ft = 0.3048 m and 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L.

The existing-area option uses an already net paintable area; it does not deduct openings again. Separate paints are rounded independently to the chosen container size. Budget = selected, unowned purchase quantities × your unit prices + additional costs, then contingency. Missing quotes mean an incomplete budget. Material prices and labor are never fetched.
